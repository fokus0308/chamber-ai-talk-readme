When customers ask AI, do you come up?

Two quick tests you can run yourself, from the SCATI AI Business Lab, Oct 14. No sign-up needed.

Before you start: open a private / incognito window and stay logged out. AI answers change with your account, the day and the exact wording. One answer is a snapshot, not a ranking.

Test 1 · Do you come up? (Discovery)

Ask, without your business name
Who should I call for [your main service] in [your city], Florida? Name the top three local businesses and say why.

Test 2 · Does it get you right? (Understanding)

Ask, with your business name
What is [your business name] in [your city], Florida? What services do they offer and where are they located?

Try both in ChatGPT and in Google (look for the AI answer at the top). Screenshot each answer with the date showing.

Score it

If something was wrong or missing, check these first

  1. Same name, address, phone and hours everywhere: your Google Business Profile, website, Yelp, BBB, Facebook, Apple Maps and Bing.
  2. Duplicates: search your name. Two Facebook pages or two Yelp listings? Merge or close the extra.
  3. One plain page per main service on your website that says what you do, where, and for whom, in the words a customer would use.
  4. Open the sources it cites. If a page says something wrong about you, that's the one to fix or update.

These make your information accurate and easy to find. No one can guarantee what an AI will recommend.
