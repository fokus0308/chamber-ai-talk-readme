# Speaking notes: "When Your Customers Ask AI, Do You Come Up?"
SCATI AI Business Lab · Wed Oct 14 · 8:00 AM · Buena Vida Estates

QR code and short URL on screen from the start: **palmbaymarketingseo.com/ai-check**

## 1 · Customer question
- "Who here has asked AI to recommend a business?"
- Two tests: **Discovery** (ask for the service, not the name) and **Understanding** (ask about the business by name).
- Show the question: *"Who should I call for kitchen hood fire suppression in Melbourne, Brevard County, Florida? Name the top three local businesses and say why."*

## 2 · Saved answer
- Label it on screen: *Claude with web search, saved Sep 14 and Oct 1* (or ChatGPT, logged out, Oct 13, once captured).
- Discovery: in both saved answers to this question, Patriot wasn't among the businesses named. Six different businesses across the two dates.
- Understanding: asked by name, it described Patriot correctly, kitchen hood work included.
- Contrast: emergency lighting in Melbourne. Patriot was recommended first in the Oct 1 saved answer.

## 3 · Supporting source
- Show the cited page(s) from the capture: what the AI read about Patriot.
- Show whether Patriot's site has a page about kitchen hood suppression in Melbourne *(only what the capture verified)*.

## 4 · Lesson
- "Being known and being recommended are separate tests. We can see what it said and where it looked. We can't see why it chose."

## 5 · Practical action
- *(Only if verified)* one plain page per main service and city, in the customer's words.
- Audience: phones out, private window, run both tests on your own business (5 min). Questions.
- Close: "Everything's on that QR code, no sign-up needed. If you want me to run a visibility map, the request form is at the bottom of the page. I review each one myself and email you when it's ready."

## Fallback sentence (if the live answer changes)
> "AI answers change by day, by wording and by tool, which is exactly why I saved these with dates. What matters is the pattern you can check, not any single answer."
