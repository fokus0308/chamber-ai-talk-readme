3. Summit Fire & Security — Melbourne branch A larger company with a dedicated Melbourne location. They offer a range of fire protection services from fire sprinkler inspection to kitchen fire suppression system repair, covering Palm Bay, Titusville, Cocoa, and Merritt Island. Good choice if you have multiple locations or want a bigger outfit with broader after-hours coverage.

A couple of practical notes before you call:

- Florida requires hood suppression systems to be inspected every six months by a licensed contractor. Ask for the UL-300 compliance status of your system up front.
- Verify the company holds a valid Class D license from the State Fire Marshal and that the technician on site carries a valid Class 4 permit — that's the same standard Brevard County Public Schools requires of its hood-system vendors, so it's a reasonable bar for you too.
- Hood *cleaning* and hood *suppression* are different services with different licenses. If you need both, confirm the company does both or ask for a referral.
